Food / Catering

Sponsor coffee breaks and lunches during the conference. The daily food and beverage cost for one delegate is on average 150 - 170 TRY. Considering our 3-day program, the average cost per delegate is 450 - 510 TRY.

Printing & Materials

We provide each delegate with 1 tote bag, 1 pen, 1 notebook, 1 folder, 1 placard, and 1 badge for the conference. According to our market research, the average cost of this fully equipped kit per delegate is around 100 - 130 TRY.

Transportation & Logistics

Covers the transfer expenses of our participants coming from within and outside Lüleburgaz. According to current data, while the shuttle/minibus transportation cost for local delegates is 100 - 150 TRY per delegate, the bus expenses for out-of-town delegates (e.g., Istanbul-Lüleburgaz round trip) average 750 - 900 TRY.
