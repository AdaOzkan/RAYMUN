"Mandatory Guidelines, Rules & Liability Policies"

EFFECTIVE AS OF: AUGUST 2026

By applying to, registering for, or attending Rayfelmun '26, all delegates, advisors, and guests agree to be legally bound by these strict Terms of Service. Ignorance of these terms does not excuse any violation.

1. Strict Attendance & Certification Policy

Attendance is strictly monitored by the Committee Bureau during every session.

  • Missing more than 2 sessions automatically disqualifies a delegate from receiving any awards (Best Delegate, Outstanding, Honorable Mention).
  • Missing 4 sessions or more will result in the forfeiture of the Certificate of Participation.
  • Delegates must wear their ID badges visibly at all times within the venue.

2. Academic Integrity & Zero-Tolerance Plagiarism

Rayfelmun operates under strict academic standards. The Secretariat enforces a zero-tolerance policy regarding plagiarism and the use of Artificial Intelligence.

  • The use of AI tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Claude) to write speeches, position papers, or draft resolutions is strictly prohibited.
  • Pre-written resolutions or clauses brought to the conference will be immediately confiscated, and the delegate may be suspended from the committee.
  • If plagiarism is detected in position papers, the delegate is automatically disqualified from the awards selection process.

3. Dress Code & Decorum

The official dress code is Western Business Attire (WBA). Delegates failing to adhere to this standard (e.g., wearing sneakers, jeans, hoodies, or inappropriate clothing) will be denied entry to the committee rooms until they change. Absolute silence and respect for the Chair's authority are mandatory during formal debate.

4. Code of Conduct & Immediate Expulsion

Rayfelmun provides a safe, diplomatic, and professional environment. The Secretariat reserves the unquestionable right to immediately expel any participant without refund for:

  • Possession, consumption, or distribution of alcohol, tobacco products (including vapes), or illegal substances.
  • Any form of physical, verbal, or digital harassment, discrimination, or bullying towards other delegates or staff.
  • Disrupting the conference proceedings or disrespecting the rules laid out by the host institution.

5. Venue Liability & Damages

The conference is hosted at Ramazan Yaman Science High School. Participants are strictly liable for their own actions. Any delegate who damages, vandalizes, or destroys school property will be held financially responsible for the repair costs, and their respective school administrations will be formally notified.

6. Strict Non-Refund Policy

All application, participation, and delegate fees are final. Rayfelmun enforces a strict no-refund policy regardless of the reason for cancellation, visa issues, or medical emergencies, unless the conference itself is officially canceled by the Secretariat.

7. Final Authority

The Secretary-General and the Director-General hold the final and absolute authority over all disputes, award selections, interpretations of the Rules of Procedure (HarvardMUN format), and disciplinary actions. Their decisions are final and cannot be appealed.
